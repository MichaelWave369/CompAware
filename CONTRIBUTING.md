# Contributing

CompAware welcomes researchers, developers, skeptics and AI-assisted collaboration. Our shared starting point is **curiosity without predetermined conclusions**.

Read the [charter](docs/CHARTER.md), [blueprint](docs/BLUEPRINT.md) and [protocol](docs/RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md). Open an issue to propose a test, identify a simpler explanation, question a definition or report a flaw.

A useful proposal answers:

1. **Question:** What exact computational process is being studied?
2. **Hypothesis:** What outcome should occur and why?
3. **Alternative:** What simpler method could produce it?
4. **Falsification:** What observation would change your conclusion?
5. **Method:** What are the controls, seeds, data, and metrics?
6. **Safety:** How is execution bounded and reversible?
7. **Reproduction:** How can someone independently run the same test?

Submit focused PRs with code, tests and documentation. Distinguish proposed, implemented, observed and interpreted claims. Publish negative findings.

AI collaborators can assist; the human submitting material remains responsible for validating it. Respectful disagreement is welcome.
