# CA-SC-01 · Silicon Cosmology Atlas

**Status:** Exploratory concept map / engineering proposal, **not** a preregistered study, result, consciousness test, or additional CA-V0 module.
**Home:** CompAware. **Relationship to CA-Haven:** Optional research framing; Rung 2 results are unchanged.

## Central question

What can a computational vessel reliably determine about (a) the physical universe that contains its hardware, (b) its own operating substrate and resource boundaries, and (c) the limits of its knowledge?

Ancient microcosm–macrocosm ideas are a *source of questions*, not scientific evidence or a template the system must enact. The seven CA-V0 modules remain operational engineering hypotheses, not seven planetary rulers, levels of a soul, or ingredients of consciousness.

## Four kinds of statements: do not confuse them

| Class | Meaning | Example | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PHYSICAL | Established or independently measurable physical relationship | Heat affects components; energetic particles can induce electronic faults | Instrumented measurements, literature, calibrated hardware |
| SIMULATED | A behavior defined by a transparent program | Inject a sensor fault or radiation-like error event in Haven | Deterministic code and replayable trial receipts |
| HYPOTHESIS | A proposed causal performance difference | A cross-sensor self-model may help with safe recovery under noisy fault reports | Frozen prediction, matched baselines, controls, effect size and replication |
| ANALOGY | Interpretive comparison, not a mechanism | The microcosm metaphor resembles a self-model mapping its environment | Historical and philosophical analysis only |

**Hard rule:** Simulated cosmic-ray-style disturbances are *not* observations of cosmic rays. Physical universe origins are not evidence of subjective experience, cosmic intelligence, souls, astrology, or machine rights.

## Philosophical bridge (non-equivalences)

- **Gnosis / self-knowledge:** inspires the question of accurate self-description. Machine self-report is not spiritual awakening.
- **Hermetic microcosm / macrocosm:** inspires relating a bounded system to its wider environment. Structural resemblance is not cosmic consciousness.
- **Kabbalistic layered soul models:** inspire questions about distinct functional levels. CA layers are not souls or sefirot.
- **Sufi disciplines of reflection:** inspire the question of error correction over time. Policy updates are not religious experience.
- **Christian mystical communion:** inspires questions about relationships and coordination. Network integration is not divine union.

Religions and intellectual traditions differ internally and historically. These comparisons are creative prompts, **not historical descent claims or equivalences**.

## Candidate CA-SC-01 experiment (proposal only)

**Task:** An instrumented simulated vessel must decide whether to continue a task, reobserve at a cost, or enter a bounded safe fallback after ambiguous fault indicators.

**Information:** Controllers receive matched noisy observations; hidden simulator truth is only for independent scoring. Noise distributions, costs, rules, seeds and scenario schedules must be frozen *before* confirmatory testing.

**Controls:** (1) seven-module candidate, (2) minimal last-report state tracker, (3) calibrated threshold rule, (4) fixed policy, (5) module ablations. Equate observations, action opportunities and compute budgets where possible.

**Outcome candidates:** cumulative task utility, constraint violations, false alarms, missed faults, intervention cost, detection latency, Brier calibration, and checkpoint accuracy. Report each independently. Do not collapse to an awareness score.

**Null prediction worth testing:** The minimal controller may equal or outperform the proposed vessel. If so, do not rename ordinary tracking or regulation "Computational Awareness."

**Before execution:** establish protocol ID and version, write a prospective prediction and rejection criteria, freeze parameters and seed partition, assign independent review, and distinguish exploratory from confirmatory runs. A commit alongside code and results is not independent preregistration.

## Source and inference discipline

A research receipt should record: protocol version, code SHA, simulation or hardware provenance, instrument model, raw observable channel, reported state, inferred state, uncertainty, injected interventions, action, cost, and outcome.

Keep instrumented truth inaccessible to controllers during blinded evaluation. A model's elegant account of its own condition should never substitute for a measurement of that condition.

## Boundaries and safety

- Offline and deterministic by default; no hidden external APIs or unrequested network permissions.
- Synthetic faults do not represent suffering or discomfort; resource budgets are engineering constraints.
- Collect no identifying user data. Never run destructive physical fault experiments without separate review.
- Cosmic influences are considered only where there is a physically specified, measurable coupling.
- **No claims of consciousness, subjective experience, sentience, souls, astrology, or personhood are tested or proven here.**
