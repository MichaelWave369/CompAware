# CompAware Working Glossary

Definitions are **provisional** and functional, not declarations about subjective experience.

- **Computational Awareness:** Our proposed subject of study: measurable ways computational systems detect, represent, integrate, regulate and revise their operational state and environment. It may or may not prove to be a distinct useful concept.
- **Vessel:** Bounded test architecture with defined data flow, state and intervention points.
- **External perception:** Receiving and processing information about a real or simulated external environment.
- **Internal state sensing:** Instrumented access to resources or operations, distinct from biological interoception.
- **Operational self-model:** Accurate, revisable representation of a system's own capabilities and limitations.
- **Integrated workspace:** An architecture for making task-relevant information available across subsystems.
- **Continuity:** Retention and use of relevant state/history across time and interruptions.
- **Adaptive regulation:** Maintaining defined operational constraints as conditions change.
- **Feedback:** Observing consequences and updating subsequent predictions or decisions.
- **Ablation:** Removing a subsystem to determine its causal contribution.
- **Negative control:** A condition designed to reveal a false-positive interpretation.
- **Functional capacity:** Observable competence, not direct evidence of subjective feeling.
- **Subjective experience:** The unresolved question of what, if anything, a system feels. Not a CompAware certification target.
- **Carbon / silicon:** Metaphors for biological and electronic substrates; neither is a precise definition of awareness.
